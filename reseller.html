Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our IPTV reseller program.

What is an IPTV reseller?

＋

An IPTV reseller purchases credits that can be used to provide IPTV subscriptions to customers as part of their reseller business.

How do reseller credits work?

＋

Each reseller plan includes a specific number of credits. Credits can be used according to the reseller system to activate customer subscriptions.

Which reseller plan should I choose?

＋

Choose a plan based on the number of customers you expect to serve. You can start with 10 credits and scale to larger packages as your business grows.

Can I buy more credits later?

＋

Yes, resellers can expand their business by purchasing additional reseller credits when needed.

Is IPTV reselling suitable for beginners?

＋

Yes. The reseller model can be a simple way to start an online subscription business, provided you comply with all applicable laws and only resell properly licensed/authorized services.
